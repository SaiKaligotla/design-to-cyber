  * TAC Security (Mohali / Mumbai)

  * Safe Security / Lucideus (Delhi NCR / Bengaluru)
* **B2B SaaS / Infrastructure Startups:**
  * Zoho, Freshworks, BrowserStack, Postman, Whatfix, Chargebee, HackerEarth.

### Resume & Portfolio Positioning
* Highlight your 3+ years of UI/UX design: complex data workflows, admin dashboards, forms, permission controls, and design systems.
* Mention your **Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate** under Education/Certifications. It signals to tech startup founders that you understand the domain their products operate in.

### The 12-Month Internal Job Posting (IJP) Playbook
1. **Months 1–6:** Focus 100% on crushing your design targets. Build rock-solid rapport with product managers and developers.
2. **Months 6–9:** Reach out to the internal InfoSec / SecOps lead. Offer to help them design:
   * Company-wide Anti-Phishing awareness emails and infographics.
   * Executive security reporting slide decks.
   * Internal security tooling UI improvements.
3. **Months 9–12:** When an internal vacancy opens in Security Operations, Application Security, GRC, or Product Security, apply via **IJP**. Internal transfers bypass external HR ATS screening and evaluate your proven performance and culture fit.

## 3. Track 2: Direct Cyber via Tier-2 Boutique MSSPs

### Target Companies
* Sequretek, Inspira Enterprise, Netenrich, Certcube Labs, CyArt, Whizhack Technologies, CyberEye, Innefu Labs.
* Avoid applying via standard portals at TCS/Infosys/Wipro, where automated screening immediately rejects non-STEM degrees.

### What to Prepare (100% Free)
1. **Networking Fundamentals:**
   * OSI 7-layer model vs TCP/IP.
   * DNS resolution steps (Root → TLD → Authoritative).
   * Key ports: 22 (SSH), 53 (DNS), 80 (HTTP), 443 (HTTPS), 3389 (RDP), 445 (SMB).
2. **Log Triage (The #1 Interview Test):**
   * Windows Security Event IDs:
     * **4624:** Successful Logon
     * **4625:** Failed Logon (Brute Force indicator)
     * **4688:** Process Creation
     * **4720:** User Account Created
   * How to query logs in Splunk or Elastic: Searching by IP, time range, and event code.
3. **Google Cybersecurity Certificate Talking Points:**
   * Be ready to walk the interviewer through the hands-on labs you completed in the certificate: analyzing packet captures with Wireshark, basic Linux commands (`grep`, `cat`, `chmod`), and incident report writing.

## 4. Weekly Milestone Breakdown (Weeks 1 to 12)

| Week | Focus Area | Actionable Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weeks 1–2** | Resume & Portfolio Polishing | Create two tailored resumes: (1) Senior B2B Tech Product Designer, (2) Junior Security Analyst / SOC Trainee (highlighting Google Cyber Cert). Set up job alerts on Instahyre, Wellfound, and LinkedIn. |
| **Weeks 3–4** | Technical Core Review (Networking & Logs) | Review Professor Messer's free CompTIA Security+ videos for networking and threats. Practice inspecting Windows Event Viewer logs on your Windows 11 PC. |
| **Weeks 5–6** | Hands-On SIEM Practice (Free) | Download Splunk Enterprise (free local license) and ingest the free Boss of the SOC (BOTS) dataset. Practice searching for failed logins and suspicious PowerShell commands. |
| **Weeks 7–8** | High-Volume Application Blitz | Submit 15–20 targeted applications per week across both tracks. Connect directly with startup founders, design managers, and security managers on LinkedIn. |
| **Weeks 9–10** | Mock Interviews & Scenario Drills | Practice answering common Indian interview questions: *"Walk me through the lifecycle of a phishing incident,"* *"Explain DNS,"* and *"How do you detect brute force attacks?"* |
| **Weeks 11–12** | Offer Evaluation & Onboarding | Review offers, negotiate based on current market rates, and accept your entry role into the tech ecosystem. |
