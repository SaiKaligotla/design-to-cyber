## 3. The 16-Week Accelerated Syllabus (30 Hours/Week)

```
Month 1: Technical & Protocol De-mystification (Security Foundations)
Month 2: Threat Modeling, Authentication Architecture & Privacy Laws
Month 3: Capstone Case Study 1 (Enterprise Security Dashboard / Falcon/Prisma Redesign)
Month 4: Capstone Case Study 2 (DPDP Act 2023 Consent & Passkey Authentication Flow)
Month 5-6: Indian Market Launch, Portfolio Presentation & Targeted Outreach
```

### Month 1: Core Systems & Networking Foundations (Weeks 1–4)
*Goal: Understand the underlying technical architecture so developers and security engineers can never dismiss your designs as technically unfeasible.*

- **Week 1: The Machine & Local Virtualization**
  - Concepts: OS Kernels, Userland vs Kernel space, Process isolation, POSIX permissions (`chmod 770`), Windows NTFS ACLs.
  - Practice: Set up your Windows 11 host-only virtual network with an Ubuntu/Fedora VM (no router needed).
- **Week 2: The Network & The Web**
  - Concepts: TCP/IP handshake, DNS resolution, Ports (22, 53, 80, 443, 3389), HTTP/S request/response cycle, Headers, Cookies (`SameSite`, `HttpOnly`, `Secure` flags).
  - Practice: Use browser DevTools (Network tab) to trace authentication requests and session tokens.
- **Week 3: Core Cryptography for Designers**
  - Concepts: Symmetric encryption (AES) vs Asymmetric encryption (RSA/ECC), Hashing (SHA-256) vs Salting, Digital Signatures, Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) and SSL/TLS certificates.
  - Practice: Generate and test SSH Ed25519 keypairs between Windows and Linux.
- **Week 4: The Threat Landscape & The Human Vulnerability**
  - Concepts: The CIA Triad, STRIDE threat modeling, Social Engineering (Phishing, Vishing, Smishing), MFA Fatigue attacks, Dark Patterns vs Deceptive Designs.
  - Free Resources: SANS Security Awareness papers, Professor Messer CompTIA Security+ (Sy0-701) YouTube series (Sections 1 & 2).

### Month 2: Authentication, Privacy Laws & Human Factors (Weeks 5–8)
*Goal: Master modern identity architecture, privacy regulations, and cognitive psychology in security.*

- **Week 5: Identity, Authentication & Modern Protocols**
  - Concepts: Passwords vs Passkeys (FIDO2 / WebAuthn), Multi-Factor Authentication (TOTP, Push, Biometrics, SMS vulnerabilities), Single Sign-On (SSO), OAuth 2.0 vs OpenID Connect (OIDC).
  - Deliverable: Flowchart breakdown of an OAuth 2.0 authorization code flow highlighting user consent moments.
- **Week 6: Privacy by Design & India's DPDP Act 2023**
  - Concepts: Principles of data minimization, purpose limitation, storage limitation.
  - Legal deep dive: The Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) 2023:
    - Section 5 (Notice and Consent in 22 scheduled languages).
    - Section 6 (Consent Managers framework).
    - Section 9 (Verifiable parental consent for minors).
    - Section 11-13 (User Rights: access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal).
- **Week 7: Usable Security & Friction Balancing**
  - Concepts: Why users bypass security controls; warning fatigue; interstitial dialog design; progressive disclosure; step-up authentication triggers based on risk context (device, IP, velocity).
- **Week 8: Threat Modeling a User Journey**
  - Concepts: Mapping user journeys against threat actors. Applying STRIDE to an onboarding workflow.

### Month 3: Portfolio Capstone 1 — Enterprise Cyber SaaS Redesign (Weeks 9–12)
*Goal: Build an unassailable case study proving you can design complex security tooling.*

- **Project:** **"Incident Triage & Alert Fatigue Mitigation in an Enterprise SIEM/XDR Console"**
- **The Problem:** SOC analysts suffer from extreme cognitive overload and alert fatigue looking at dense tabular log data, causing real intrusions to be missed.
- **Your Solution:**
  1. User Research: Synthesize common cognitive failure points during alert triage.
  2. Information Architecture: Redesign the alert hierarchy (grouping by MITRE ATT&CK technique, contextual entity correlation).
  3. Actionable UX: Design 1-click guided response playbooks (e.g., "Isolate Host", "Revoke Token", "Block IP") with built-in safety confirmation modals to prevent accidental enterprise outages.
- **Deliverable:** High-fidelity Figma prototype + comprehensive case study writeup.

### Month 4: Portfolio Capstone 2 — DPDP Act 2023 Privacy & Passkey Onboarding (Weeks 13–16)
*Goal: Directly target the most urgent regulatory need in Indian FinTech and consumer tech.*

- **Project:** **"DPDP 2023-Compliant Privacy Center & Frictionless Passkey Onboarding for an Indian FinTech"**
- **The Problem:** Indian users either blindly accept terms or drop off during complex 2FA onboarding; compliance teams force ugly legal jargon that harms conversion rates.
- **Your Solution:**
  1. Multilingual Notice Architecture: Designing a clear, tiered privacy notice compliant with Section 5 of the DPDP Act.
  2. Granular Consent Manager: Interactive toggles for marketing vs core processing vs third-party sharing.
  3. Passkey (FIDO2) Onboarding: Designing the educational transition from OTP/Password to biometrics/passkeys on Android and iOS devices, including cross-device synchronization and account recovery.
- **Deliverable:** Complete interactive Figma design system and documentation ready for presentation to CISOs, Product Heads, and Legal teams.
