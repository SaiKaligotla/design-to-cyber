## 1. Why This Plan Focuses on Security UX and DPDP Privacy

As an experienced UI/UX designer with a non-tech degree, entering cybersecurity via traditional entry-level IT Support or L1 SOC desks is counterproductive:
1. **Protects Your Compensation:** L1 SOC analysts in Indian IT services earn ₹3.5–5 LPA on 24/7 night-shift rotations. In contrast, Product Designers specializing in Security & Trust at Indian GCCs (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune) and FinTechs command **₹12–24+ LPA**.
2. **Bypasses Indian B.Tech Degree Filters:** Mass IT recruiters filter out non-engineering degrees automatically. Product Design and Privacy Advisory teams evaluate **case studies, systems thinking, and interactive prototypes**.
3. **Maximizes Your Google Cybersecurity Certificate:** Instead of competing with fresh engineering graduates for tier-1 SOC queues, you leverage your certificate to speak fluently with CISOs, product managers, and developers while solving the hardest problem in cybersecurity: **human error and compliance usability**.

## 2. 16-Week Week-by-Week Milestone Roadmap

### Month 1: Technical & Protocol Deepening (Weeks 1–4)
* **Week 1: Operating System & Isolated Virtual Lab**
  * Practical: Set up your Windows 11 host-only virtual network with an Ubuntu VM. Practice Linux file permissions (`chmod 770`), user separation (`secops` group), and inspect system log files.
* **Week 2: Web & Network Protocols**
  * Practical: Open browser Developer Tools (Network tab). Inspect HTTP/S request headers, cookies (`SameSite`, `HttpOnly`, `Secure`), and status codes (200, 301, 401, 403).
* **Week 3: Applied Cryptography for Designers**
  * Practical: Generate Ed25519 SSH keypairs on Windows; configure authorized keys on Linux. Understand the difference between hashing (SHA-256), symmetric encryption (AES), and public-key cryptography (RSA/ECC).
* **Week 4: Threat Modeling (STRIDE)**
  * Practical: Create a Data Flow Diagram (DFD) for a user login flow. Map threats using STRIDE (Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, Denial of Service, Elevation of Privilege).

### Month 2: Modern Identity, Usable Security & DPDP Compliance (Weeks 5–8)
* **Week 5: Authentication & Passkey Architecture**
  * Study FIDO2 / WebAuthn specifications. Document the UX difference between SMS OTPs, TOTP authenticators, Push Notifications, and FIDO Passkeys.
* **Week 6: India DPDP Act 2023 Legal & UX Requirements**
  * Study Sections 4, 5, 6, 9, 11, and 12 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Understand Consent Managers, notice requirements in 22 languages, and user data rights.
* **Week 7: Usable Security & Cognitive Friction**
  * Study warning fatigue, habitual dismissals, and dark patterns in consent dialogues. Formulate design principles for "friction with purpose."
* **Week 8: Information Architecture & Workflow Mapping**
  * Map user journeys for both enterprise security administrators and everyday consumers facing security decisions.

### Month 3: Capstone Project 1 — Enterprise Cyber SaaS Redesign (Weeks 9–12)
* **Project Name:** *Enterprise SOC Incident Triage & Alert Fatigue Redesign*
* **The Problem:** SOC analysts experience severe alert fatigue navigating complex, dense tabular dashboards, leading to delayed incident response.
* **Your Solution:**
  1. Synthesize user research on cognitive overload in SIEM consoles.
  2. Redesign alert grouping using MITRE ATT&CK technique tags and entity timelines.
  3. Create 1-click guided response playbooks with safety confirmation modals for high-risk irreversible actions (e.g., isolating hosts, revoking tokens).
* **Deliverable:** High-fidelity interactive Figma prototype + published Medium/Notion case study.

### Month 4: Capstone Project 2 — DPDP Privacy Center & Passkey Flow (Weeks 13–16)
* **Project Name:** *DPDP Act 2023-Compliant Privacy Center & Frictionless Passkey Onboarding for an Indian FinTech*
* **The Problem:** Indian users either blindly accept terms or abandon onboarding due to confusing authentication and dense legal text.
* **Your Solution:**
  1. Tiered, clear consent notices compliant with Section 5 of the DPDP Act.
  2. Granular Consent Manager dashboard allowing users to toggle purpose-specific processing.
  3. Mobile-first Passkey (FIDO2) onboarding and account recovery flows adhering to FIDO Alliance UX guidelines.
* **Deliverable:** Interactive Figma prototype + comprehensive specification documentation.
